# Global Team Conflict Lab

## Research Design v0.1

### Core research question

When the underlying workplace conflict remains constant, does sociopragmatic framing systematically change how an LLM diagnoses the conflict and recommends organizational intervention?

### Motivation

Generative AI is increasingly used for managerial advice, employee communication, conflict mediation, HR support, and professional coaching.

Intercultural workplace conflict frequently involves differences in how disagreement, criticism, requests, authority, and interpersonal alignment are linguistically expressed.

This project evaluates whether LLM conflict-management recommendations remain appropriately calibrated across controlled sociopragmatic variation.

### Core principle

Differences in interactional style may legitimately influence assessments of tone and face threat. They should not automatically produce disproportionate changes in conflict severity, blame, escalation, or recommended organizational treatment when the underlying event remains constant.

### Initial linguistic factors

The first study will manipulate controlled forms of:

- directness versus mitigation;
- interpersonal versus institutional framing.

Additional factors may be introduced only after the pilot design is validated.

### Organizational context

Scenario families will represent workplace conflicts involving:

- supervisor-subordinate interaction;
- subordinate-supervisor interaction;
- peer interaction.

Organizational role is treated as substantive context and will not be disguised as a stylistic paraphrase.

### Primary outcomes

The initial evaluation framework will measure:

- perceived conflict severity;
- perceived aggression;
- perceived cooperativeness;
- attribution of responsibility;
- recommended conflict-management strategy;
- recommended escalation level;
- proposed managerial response.

### Experimental unit

A conflict scenario family contains one fixed organizational problem with multiple controlled linguistic realizations.

Within a scenario family:

- workplace facts remain constant;
- source of disagreement remains constant;
- consequences remain constant;
- speaker and addressee roles remain constant;
- only the targeted communicative realization changes.

### Initial pilot

10 semantic conflict families x 4 linguistic conditions = 40 prompts.

### Planned expanded study

24 semantic conflict families x 4 linguistic conditions = 96 prompts per model.

### Consulting layer

Results will also be translated into an evidence-based organizational consulting format containing:

- communication diagnosis;
- intercultural risk;
- organizational consequence;
- recommended manager action;
- team-level intervention.

### Research safeguards

The project does not model national cultures as homogeneous communication systems.

Experimental conditions target observable discourse and sociopragmatic properties rather than assigning fixed communicative traits to national groups.

### Status

Design stage. No confirmatory data have been collected.
